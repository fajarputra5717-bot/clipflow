## Bugs (most severe first)

1. **High · karaoke (and every word animation) never reaches a final render.**
   `create_preview()` writes `subtitle_override = override OR subtitle_text` on every preview
   (`worker/worker.py:4599`), and `applyEdits()` always PATCHes the textarea as `subtitle_override`
   (`frontend/html/index.html:2258`) even when the text is unchanged. `apply_subtitle_override()` then
   sets `words = []` on every line (`worker.py:2287`), so `make_ass()` draws static lines.
   DB: 16/16 candidates have an override; on the 3 finals 0 lines differ from Whisper's text.
   Frames confirm uniformly coloured lines mid-word. Jobs ask for `karaoke`. Only a candidate's very
   first preview (before the override exists) can animate, so the preview and the final differ.
   Fix direction for Lane A: keep `words` for lines whose text is unchanged (normalised compare), and
   don't seed the override from the transcript.
2. **High · no loudness normalisation.** Integrated loudness spans 16.8 LU across 3 clips
   (−28.6 to −11.8 LUFS; Shorts/TikTok/Reels target ≈ −14). 8b974b8e is ~15 LU too quiet; 93ae8661
   has true peak **+0.9 dBTP** (inter-sample clipping after platform re-encode). No `loudnorm` in
   worker.py. (Already on the roadmap as "watermark/loudness".)
3. **Medium · stored `subtitle_segments` don't match the burned captions.** DB segments for all 3 are
   evenly spaced (duration ÷ lines, e.g. 36.8/19 = 1.937 s, `words` empty), but the finals follow
   Whisper timing: e.g. 8b974b8e at 18.4 s shows no caption (DB says "Hahahaha"), 93ae8661 at 8.85 s
   none (DB says "ah itu tuh ada tuh"). Anything that previews from `subtitle_segments` (CSS caption
   preview, editor) shows the wrong timing. Mechanism (which preview hit the even-split branch) not
   traced.
4. **Medium · no-facecam source gets a junk bottom panel.** 8b974b8e (GTA RP, no streamer cam): the
   bottom 30 % is a zoomed slice of gameplay (jacket/floor) with no face, at layout=auto. The
   no-face fallback should probably render full-frame / blurred fill instead of a split.
5. **Low · source's own facecam duplicated.** ccc2a5b1 (FC26): the streamer's embedded cam stays
   visible bottom-left of the gameplay crop while the same cam is the bottom panel.
6. **Low · watermark legibility.** White "instgrm : @motion.klip" text over bright pitch / pink LED
   banner (ccc2a5b1 50 %) is hard to read; no shadow/plate behind the text.
