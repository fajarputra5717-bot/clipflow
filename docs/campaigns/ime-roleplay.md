Clipping Tips 
Permainkan judul video, tidak harus selalu menyebut IME Roleplay secara langsung. Fokus utama adalah hook di awal clip yang menarik perhatian.
Ambil momen IC yang seru, lucu, tegang, atau unexpected, lalu kreasikan sesuai kreativitas kalian untuk menarik algoritma dan meningkatkan views.
Contoh Judul Menarik:

* "Gak Nyangka Endingnya Begini..."
* "Day 1 Main RP" - "Day 1 Dikejar...", dan lain sebagainya
* "(MK) Momen Ketika..."
* "Anomali di RP..."

Pilih judulmu dan berkreasilah sebaik mungkin agar views meningkat!

## Questions to the campaign admin

Answers marked *answered by admin 2026-10-02* replace the earlier working assumptions.

1. Weeks W1–W4 end on 28 October and the budget is exactly 4 refills. Are uploads on 29–31 October
   eligible? **Not eligible.** *answered by admin 2026-10-02*
2. Is the 40.000-view target per single post (one platform), or summed across platforms?
   **Per post on a single platform, not summed.** *answered by admin 2026-10-02*
3. Does one clip posted to several platforms count as one video or one per platform?
   **One per platform: the same clip on 6 platforms counts as 6 videos.** *answered by admin 2026-10-02*
4. Is "1 akun" one creator, or each platform account separately? **Each platform account
   separately: max 2 eligible videos per month per platform account.** *answered by admin 2026-10-02*
   - Cap per creator with one account per platform: 2 videos × 6 platform accounts × Rp 200.000 =
     **Rp 2.400.000/month**.
   - Still open: whether a creator may run several accounts on the SAME platform (not assumed).
5. When a week's refill (Rp 5.000.000 = 25 videos) runs out: **first come, first served.**
   *answered by admin 2026-10-02*
   - Still open: does unused weekly budget carry over to the next week? (not assumed)
